# On a statement of A. N. Shoshitaishvili about weighted homogeneous singularities

Alex Shoshitaishvili (also published as A. N. Shoshitaishvili), Independent Researcher

## Summary

Statement 1 of the author's 1976 paper (Funct. Anal. Appl. 10:2, 1976) concerns a weighted
homogeneous polynomial F with an isolated critical point. Its second part is equivalent to the
absence of nonzero derivations of negative weight of the Milnor algebra of F; since 1995 it has
been studied as a conjecture of S. S.-T. Yau, and as the Milnor-algebra case of Halperin's
conjecture.

The preprint describes the history of the statement and proves: with
M = min(w_3 - 2w_n, w_1 - 3w_n), the Milnor algebra has no nonzero derivation of weight -q if
q > M, or if q > M - (w_{n-1} - w_n) and w_n does not divide d - q. In particular it has no
nonzero derivation of negative weight when w_3 <= 2w_n or w_1 <= 3w_n. This follows from a
row-by-row criterion (Theorem 6.1).
